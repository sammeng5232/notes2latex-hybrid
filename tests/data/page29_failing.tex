\underline{Orientation}

\textit{Def.} $M^n C^\infty$ mfd. Two charts $(U_\alpha, \chi_\alpha, \varphi_\alpha), (U_\beta, \chi_\beta, \varphi_\beta)$ are \underline{orientation compatible} if $\det(d\varphi_{\alpha\beta}) > 0 \forall x \in \chi_\alpha(U_\alpha \cap U_\beta)$. An \underline{orientation} of $M$ is an atlas $\mathcal{A} = \{(U_\alpha, \chi_\alpha, \varphi_\alpha)\}$ w/ pairwise orientation compatibility. $M$ is \underline{orientable} if $\exists$ orientation.

\textit{Thm.} Orientable $\Leftrightarrow \exists$ nowhere vanishing $w \in \Omega^n(M)$.

\begin{proof}
Sps $\exists$ nowhere vanishing $w \in \Omega^n(M)$. Then $\forall$ chart $(U_\alpha, \chi_\alpha, x_1, \dots, x_n), \exists f, w = f dx_{\alpha_1} \wedge \dots \wedge dx_{\alpha_n}$. Either $f > 0$ everywhere or $f < 0$ everywhere. WLOG sps $f > 0$. Consider another chart $(U_\beta, \chi_\beta, x_{\beta_1}, \dots, x_{\beta_n}), \exists g, w = g dx_{\beta_1} \wedge \dots \wedge dx_{\beta_n}$. WLOG sps $g > 0$. If $U_\alpha \cap U_\beta \neq \emptyset$, then $f dx_{\alpha_1} \wedge \dots \wedge dx_{\alpha_n} = g dx_{\beta_1} \wedge \dots \wedge dx_{\beta_n} = g \det(d\varphi_{\alpha\beta}) dx_{\alpha_1} \wedge \dots \wedge dx_{\alpha_n} \Rightarrow f = g \det(d\varphi_{\alpha\beta}) \Rightarrow \det(d\varphi_{\alpha\beta}) > 0$. Conversely, sps $\exists$ orientation $\mathcal{A}$. Then $\forall U_\alpha$, let $w_\alpha = dx_{\alpha_1} \wedge \dots \wedge dx_{\alpha_n}$. Pick a p.u. $\{\rho_\alpha\}$ wrt $\{U_\alpha\}$. Define $w = \sum_\alpha \rho_\alpha w_\alpha$. Since $\det(d\varphi_{\alpha\beta}) > 0$, $w$ nowhere vanishing.
\end{proof}
